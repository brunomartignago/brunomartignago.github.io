One dashboard, three modes

Instead of three screenshots, one set of real KPI cards switches between Detailed, Compact and Slide show. The cards slide to their new places, so the reader sees the same data trading detail for density (Compact) or focus (Slide show), which is the trade-off each mode makes for its users.

  • Plays once in view: Detailed → Compact → Slide show (~1.8s each), FLIP between layouts.
  • Reader drives: a Detailed / Compact / Slide show segmented control (aria-pressed); in Slide show, ‹ › step through the cards.
  • Reduced motion: static on Detailed with the control working. No JS: detailed.png.
