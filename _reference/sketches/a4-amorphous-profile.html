A profile that keeps changing

“We refer to the pattern as the amorphous profile because it continually evolves.” The figure draws the profile as a shape on the profile's own axes (Attributes, Density, History, Pattern, Dimensionality) and runs it through a day. As real-time signals arrive (location, time, a purchase), the shape changes, so the word “amorphous” is shown rather than explained.
