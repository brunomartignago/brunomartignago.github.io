Anatomy of a KPI card

A real card from the dashboard (Site Search Visits, 178,318) is rebuilt in HTML. When it scrolls into view it pulls apart into its three parts (heading, elements and extra information), each part tagged in the card colors. Toggling back assembles it, so the anatomy is seen on the card itself rather than with colored outlines on a screenshot.
