Cards that fit like Lego

The dashboard grid starts as empty slots in the three sizes from the structure image. KPI cards drop into them one by one, each landing with a hard-shadow lift. “Rearrange” packs a different set of cards into a new layout that still fits perfectly, which is what “insert cards of diverse sizes and content and make them fit like Lego pieces” means.
