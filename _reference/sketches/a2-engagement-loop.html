Every lap makes the profile richer

The triangle becomes a loop you can watch: a token travels Experience → Collection → Training and back, along Interaction, Transformation and Workflow. Each completed lap adds a ring of data to the Profile at the center, so “continuous engagement” reads as something that compounds, not three words on corners.
