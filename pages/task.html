The challenge

The brief

Summary

You were just hired by Company X to design a mobile product experience that makes it easy for parents and their children to track household chores. Design the experience from the perspective of the parent and the children.

The brief

Pain points

Parents

  • Regularly complain that household chores aren’t completed on time
  • Don’t know which chores have been started, and which haven’t
  • Get frustrated when their children forget regularly-repeating chores

Children

  • Don’t know when their parent expects the chore to be completed by
  • Often finish a chore, only to be told by their parents that another chore was more important
  • Don’t have an easy way to tell their parents they’ve started or finished a chore
The brief

Task

We’re looking for you to recommend flows that address the pain points for parents and children. We expect to see a user flow or information architecture that represents the UX, some lo-fidelity designs/sketches, and some hi-fidelity designs with a consistent visual identity.

My answer

The app

Tracker

the app at a glance
Object
Chores
Time-frame
Chore completion; guardians give the task’s end date
Users
Guardians and kids
Problem to be solved
Inability to keep track of current task progression, as well as create incentives for the children to follow up with tasks
Proof mechanism
Tasks that require further proof for the parents to consider done will have an area for the children to provide the specific proof
Possible edge-cases
Overdue tasks · Accidental task completion · Multiple guardians managing tasks

What the app does

for each user

Give visibility to guardians on children’s progress

  • Create new chores
  • Progression: overdue tasks
  • Progression: chores open to be done
  • Progression: chores already done

Assist children in completing their tasks

  • Task priority
  • Task completion component
  • Notifications
  • Reward system

The user journey

Two complementary experiences, each tailored for each user type, and how they will interact with the app.

The Guardian’s app includes many tools for creating and managing tasks. It's easy to follow the progress for separated kids and create and manage different types of reward systems for each kid in the household.

Home dashboard

The Guardian app also has a complete dashboard on the home page, allowing a quick check-in on progress daily.

For the kids' app, the focus is on giving them easy-to-use tools to organize their tasks, track their progress, and see how completing them affects the rewards they can earn.
